Privacy Policy

Bonita Translator · Retail Software Systems LLC · Effective September 26, 2026

Bonita Translator is a real-time English/Spanish translation app designed for retail counter use. It is built so that nothing you say leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no sign-in. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Microphone & speech

  • No recordings. Microphone audio is streamed to the on-device speech recognizer and immediately discarded. The app never writes audio to disk.
  • No uploads of speech or text. There is no code path that transmits conversation audio or translated text off the device.

Translation

Translation uses Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on-device after a one-time language-model download. The download itself contacts Google servers but carries no conversation data. Once downloaded, translation works offline.

Conversation history

  • The transcript exists only in app memory during a session.
  • An optional "keep conversation after closing" setting (off by default) stores it in on-device app storage only, capped at 200 exchanges.
  • You can delete all saved history with one tap.
  • There is no remote conversation history of any kind.

Over-the-air updates

The app uses Capgo to check for web-bundle updates at launch. Capgo receives the app identifier, bundle version, and basic device info (platform, OS version). It never sees microphone audio, transcripts, or translations — it only delivers UI code updates published from our repository.

Platform speech services

The app requests on-device speech recognition whenever the device supports it. On some older devices or unsupported language/hardware combinations, the operating system's own speech service may process audio on its servers:

StageAndroid
Speech recognition On-device on Android 12+ with Google speech services. On older devices, or if an offline model is missing, the system recognizer may send audio to Google's speech service.
Translation Google ML Kit — 100% on-device after model download.
Text-to-speech Device TTS voices (offline once installed).

This app adds no cloud service of its own. The only possible network party is the operating system's built-in speech service on older or unsupported hardware.

Permissions

  • Microphone — the only sensitive permission, used for live speech recognition.
  • Internet — used for the one-time ML Kit model download and Capgo update checks only.
  • No Bluetooth, location, camera, or contacts permissions are requested.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
